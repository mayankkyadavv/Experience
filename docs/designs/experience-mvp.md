# Design: Experience MVP (native iOS, resurfacing-first)

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-10-18
Branch: main
Repo: mayankkyadavv/Experience
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Startup

## Problem Statement

People save short-form videos (Reels, TikToks, Shorts) of things they want to do and then never do them. The founder, who is also user #1, says they "rarely actually do" anything they save, and that the reason is "I forget or can't find it." Saves have no location and nothing brings them back when the user could actually go. Experience exists to turn those saves into visits.

## Demand Evidence

- **Founder pain only.** No one else has used anything yet. The founder predicts a nearby reminder would have gotten them in the door. That prediction is untested.
- **Outside signal that the problem is common.** At least 8 apps ship share-reel-to-map (Venturr, Rhyme, Triply, glide, GeoTok at $14.99/mo, Travio, YK, the indie "Go There"). One indie founder titled their launch post "I kept DMing myself Instagram reels I never visited." That shows the problem is widely felt. It does **not** show that any of these apps get people to go; none publish visit or done rates.
- **Gap:** no evidence yet that resurfacing changes behavior. The validation plan below exists to produce it.

## Status Quo

Users tap the native Save (bookmark) in Instagram/TikTok. It costs one tap and zero thought. Saves carry no location and are never searched again. **One-tap Save is the real competitor.** Capture in Experience has to cost close to the same.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **First users:** the founder's own friend groups. They have groups in Boston, NYC, and Philadelphia. Validation uses **one** city (the densest group), with 20-30 friends plus 5-10 strangers from a local subreddit or Discord as an honest control.
- **Wedge:** not "a life backlog of everything." The wedge is whichever category of saves first turns into repeated visits. Validation records the category of every save so the data picks it (likely candidates: food/drink, bars, local activities).
- **Names:** the founder has not yet named the specific 20-30 people. Writing that list is part of the assignment.

## Constraints

- Never download or re-host platform video. Show content only through official embeds.
- Instagram oEmbed is documented as "for embedding... not for any other purpose." Meta removed the author and thumbnail fields in Nov 2025. Tokenless access (reported June 2026) covers public posts only and has lower rate limits. **Assume an Instagram URL gives no caption or geotag** until verified.
- Every field records provenance (`source` / `lookup` / `estimated` / `user`), and estimated values are labeled in the UI.
- Solo founder, pre-code, empty repo.

## Premises (agreed)

1. **Resurfacing is the core**, in two forms. *Push:* nearby nudges for everyday local use. *Pull:* a vertical scroll feed of the user's own saves, with a live mini-map that pans to each save. A feed session succeeds only if it leads to a visit, which keeps the "digital drug" feed aligned with the principle of getting people off their phones and into the world.
2. **Capture is a commodity.** It must cost about one tap. Identification accuracy is table stakes, not the moat.
3. **Validate in one city** with 20-30 friends plus 5-10 strangers. Test push first, then pull. *(Revised after the second opinion: 30 friends across 3 cities can't separate cities statistically, friends inflate done-rates out of politeness, and spreading people out kills nudge density.)* *(Amended by the approach decision: a two-week manual concierge test runs alongside the Phase 0 spikes and gates the Phase 1 build; full validation then runs on the native app via TestFlight. See "Gates" below.)*
4. **Identification is URL-first:** URL + one quick question when unsure. Platform metadata is a bonus, verified platform by platform.
5. **Validation tests the whole loop:** what people send, whether they come back to browse, and which of push vs. pull leads to visits. With ~35 users, the push-vs-pull result is **directional, not causal** (see Phase 2).

## Cross-Model Perspective

An independent Claude subagent got a summary of the session (Codex not installed). Key points:
- **Steelman:** the product turns place-intent people already leave while scrolling into real visits at the moment they're able to go. Done-visits become verified real-world taste data that capture-to-map apps don't have.
- **Most revealing answer:** "I forget or can't find it." That's a timing and retrieval problem, not an organisation problem. It flagged a High-severity tension: the founder's excitement is on the feed, the part least tied to the visit metric.
- **Challenged premise #3 (Critical):** the 3-city split. The founder accepted the revision.
- **48-hour prototype:** SMS/Telegram capture, oEmbed + LLM + Google Places identification, a Thursday-evening "3 saves near you this weekend" text, reply "done 2", a read-only Mapbox web map. It skips the native app and the feed.
- **Where we disagreed:** I argued the feed shouldn't be cut, only sequenced after push, because it's the founder's most original idea and the main thing that sets the product apart.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: Text-Bot Loop.** SMS capture + weekly nudge + web map, feed in week 3. Recommended for speed of learning; **not chosen.**
- **B: Native iOS App.** **Chosen.**
- **C: Backlog-First Feed.** Bulk import from the Instagram/TikTok data export, feed first. Not chosen as the starting point; bulk import is kept as a candidate onboarding feature (see Open Questions).

## Recommended Approach: B, Native iOS App

The founder chose native-first over the recommended text-bot loop. The plan below keeps B and orders the work so the two unknowns that could sink it get answered before the bulk of the build.

**Phase 0: Risk spikes (week 1, before the main build)**
1. **Platform metadata matrix.** For 30 of the founder's own saves (10 Instagram, 10 TikTok, 10 YouTube Shorts), record exactly what the official/legal endpoints return (oEmbed fields, title, caption, author, thumbnail, transcript availability, rate limits), and whether the place can be identified from that + external place search + at most one question. Output: a table in `docs/platforms.md`. The table decides how much the identification pipeline can rely on each platform.
2. **Embed feed spike.** A throwaway SwiftUI screen with 20 real saved posts in a vertical paging feed using official embeds (WKWebView). Measure time-to-first-frame, scroll smoothness, autoplay behavior, and memory. If it doesn't feel close to native scrolling, the feed shows a lightweight card with tap-to-play embed instead of autoplaying embeds. The card shows only fields the Phase 0 matrix confirms for that platform: place name, neighborhood, mini-map, and platform icon always; creator and thumbnail only where the platform legally provides them (expected: TikTok/YouTube yes, Instagram no). When a field is missing, the card shows a neutral platform-colored placeholder instead of an empty slot.
3. **Terms check.** Confirm in writing (from each platform's developer terms) whether oEmbed responses may be used as input to extraction, not just for display. Instagram defaults to **no** until confirmed.

**Gates**
- **Gate 1 (end of week 2): concierge result → start Phase 1.** Counted among concierge participants who forwarded at least 3 reels. **Pass:** at least 25% of them report at least one "done" in two weeks. **Rethink:** under 10%. Between 10-25%, run two more concierge weeks before building. Phase 0 spikes (including the SwiftUI embed spike) are throwaway research and run before the gate. The Phase 1 build does not start until Gate 1 passes.
- **Gate 2 (end of TestFlight week 4): validation → App Store and post-MVP work.** Uses the metrics in Success Criteria (N = active users in week 4).
  - **Fewer than 20 active users:** inconclusive. Recruit more people in the same city and run 2 more weeks before deciding. If it's still under 20 after that one extension, treat it as a kill. People not staying active is itself the signal.
  - **Pass:** both cohorts meet their targets (friends ≥1 done/active user/month, strangers ≥ half the friends' rate) → App Store launch and start Groups.
  - **Friends pass, strangers fail:** the friends' numbers are likely inflated by politeness. Run 2 more weeks with a fresh stranger group before passing. If the fresh strangers also fail, don't pass. Treat it as "works for friends only" and rethink acquisition and the value for strangers before App Store launch.
  - **Between 0.25 and target:** iterate on push (timing, copy, radius) for 2 weeks, then re-measure. If it's still in this range after that, treat it as a kill.
  - **Below 0.25:** kill (see Success Criteria).

**Phase 1: MVP build** (rough estimate: 3-4 weeks with Claude Code, then ~2-5 days of TestFlight external beta review)
- **Auth:** Sign in with Apple via Supabase Auth. The session token lives in a shared keychain access group so the share extension can submit saves as the signed-in user. If someone shares before ever signing in, the extension stores the URL in the shared App Group container and shows "Open Experience to finish setup." Queued URLs are processed right after sign-in.
- **iOS share extension** that accepts Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube URLs. The aim is that saving feels close to tapping Save: one share target, no form (except the single skippable Instagram place field below), and a confirmation the user dismisses rather than one that blocks them. Any other URL gets the message "Experience works with Instagram, TikTok and YouTube for now" and is not saved. **The confirmation is immediate and never waits for identification:** the extension uploads the URL (plus the optional Instagram place hint), shows "Saved," and closes. Every URL is written to the shared App Group outbox *before* upload. "Saved" means safely queued on the device. If the upload fails (no network, server error, expired token), the URL stays in the outbox and is retried in the background and on the next app launch. An expired token gets refreshed, or the user is asked to sign in. Nothing is removed from the outbox until the server confirms it. The app shows a "Syncing N saves" banner while the outbox isn't empty. Identification runs asynchronously on the server. Results show up in the app (the feed, map, or "Needs a place" queue). Only low-confidence results trigger the one push question, within 5 minutes.
- **Identification service** (server-side agent pipeline): signals → LLM extraction → place search → structured Experience with confidence. Per-platform inputs follow the Phase 0 matrix and terms check:
  - **TikTok/YouTube:** use any legally usable metadata (title, caption, author) → LLM → place search. Auto-pin when confidence ≥ the threshold (starting default 0.8).
  - **Instagram (committed fallback):** treat the link as URL-only. The share extension shows one inline field, "What place is this?", with place-search autocomplete (skippable). That gives Instagram one extra step: one question, aimed at under 10 seconds.
  - Log every input, output, and correction.
- **Content edge cases:**
  - *Several places in one post* ("top 5 bars"): the LLM returns a list. Each confident place becomes its own Experience linked to the same source URL. The app shows them grouped ("5 places from this reel") once identification finishes. The share confirmation is the same immediate "Saved." Places it isn't confident about go to the review queue.
  - *No fixed venue* (recipe, "go stargazing", generic activity): saved as an **idea** with no location. It shows in the list, not on the map, and isn't eligible for reminders.
  - *Chains:* store the brand plus a "multiple locations" flag. Reminders and "Go" use the nearest branch at that moment.
  - *Private or deleted posts:* save the URL and ask "What place is this?" The embed slot shows "Post unavailable."
- **Experience object** (inlined from Pitch.md): `id, user_id, title, category, location {place_name, city, country, lat, lng, place_id}, fields {price, duration, hours...} each with {value, provenance}, source {platform, url, creator}, confidence, user_confirmed, status, saved_at, done_at, source_group_id` (links multi-place saves).
  - **Provenance values:** `source` = read from the post itself (caption, geotag, transcript, visuals). `lookup` = from the places provider. `estimated` = AI inference, shown muted with an "(estimated)" label. `user` = typed or corrected by the user.
  - **What is persisted vs. fetched:**
    - *Kept permanently:* `id`, `user_id`, `title`, `category`, `place_id`, `source`, `confidence`, `user_confirmed`, `status`, timestamps, and any field with `source` or `user` provenance.
    - *Cached with expiry:* `lookup`-provenance fields (place_name, city, country, lat/lng, hours, price), held in a cache table with a separate expiry for each field, set by the chosen provider's terms (zero where caching isn't allowed), and refetched by `place_id` when expired.
    - *Recomputable at will:* `estimated` fields, regenerated as needed.
    - The digest job and reminder hours check read hours from this cache and refresh any expired entries at send time. If hours can't be fetched, the "open on Fri/Sat" ranking and the hours check are skipped for that save.
  - **Status values:** `saved`, `done`, `dismissed`, and **`needs_review`**, which is unresolved after a low-confidence result or skipped question.
- **Review queue:** `needs_review` saves live in an in-app "Needs a place" list with a badge count. They don't appear on the map and aren't eligible for reminders. The question is pushed at most once, and only if notifications are on. After that the save stays in the queue until it's resolved. There is no expiry.
  - "Not a place" turns it into a location-less idea.
  - "Something else" opens place-search autocomplete.
- **Place data storage:** storage and caching terms are a hard criterion when choosing the provider. Default design: store `place_id` permanently. Every other `lookup` field goes in the expiring cache described under "What is persisted vs. fetched," with a separate expiry for each field set by the chosen provider's terms. If the terms allow no caching for a field (for example name, hours, or price), its expiry is zero and it is fetched at display or send time. Fields the user typed (`user` provenance) are ours to keep.
- **"My Saves" feed** with live mini-map (see wireframe). Actions: **Go** (directions) and **Did it**. Estimated fields are muted and labeled. *(Eng review D2: the feed is **built during TestFlight weeks 1-2**, not in Phase 1. It ships as a TestFlight update before week 3.)*
- **Map tab:** browse, open, edit, delete.
- **Location permission and nearby reminders:**
  - Ask for *When In Use* on first launch, then ask for *Always* only after the user's 3rd resolved save, behind a screen that explains it ("so we can tell you when you're near something you saved").
  - Monitor up to 20 iOS regions: the 20 nearest saves that are resolved, have status `saved`, and haven't been reminded about in the last 30 days. Radius is 200 m.
  - Re-rank the regions on significant-location-change events and whenever the app comes to the foreground.
  - If Always is denied or only When In Use is granted, there are no geofence reminders. The user still gets the weekly digest, and the feed and map sort by distance while the app is open.
- **Weekly digest** (Thursday 6pm local time):
  - *Location reference:* the last known coarse location, captured on app open within the past 7 days. Otherwise the home neighborhood the user picks at onboarding.
  - *Which saves:* resolved saves within 3 miles of that reference. Saves open on Fri/Sat (where hours are known) rank first, then oldest saved first.
  - *Fewer than 3 qualify:* send however many qualify ("1 save near you this weekend"). If none qualify, skip that week.
- **Notification policy** (one shared cap): at most 1 per day and 3 per week across the digest and nearby reminders combined.
  - The digest uses a weekly slot first, and nearby reminders fill the rest.
  - The low-confidence question doesn't count toward the cap. It's sent only within 5 minutes of the user's own share, as a direct response.
  - Reminders respect opening hours where they're known, and never fire 10pm-9am.
- **Event logging and attribution:**
  - *Events logged with timestamps:* `reminder_sent/opened`, `digest_sent/opened`, `feed_card_viewed` (≥2 s on screen), `go_tapped`, `did_it`.
  - *Attribution:* a `did_it` is credited to the most recent touchpoint for that save within the previous 7 days. With no touchpoint in that window, it counts as `organic`. All touchpoints are kept for later analysis.
- **Privacy and data:**
  - *Stored on the server:* account ID, saves (URL plus extracted fields), corrections, event logs, and coarse location (rounded to ~1 km) used only for the digest.
  - *On device only:* precise location and geofences.
  - *Retention and deletion:* account deletion in-app removes all user data within 30 days. Logs are kept at most 12 months.
  - *Before TestFlight external testing:* a privacy policy URL, a one-screen tester consent on first launch, and location usage strings for When In Use and Always.

**Phase 2: Validation via TestFlight**
- One city: 20-30 friends + 5-10 strangers.
- Weeks 1-2: push only (nearby + weekly digest). The feed is built during these weeks and shipped behind a flag before week 3.
- Weeks 3-4: turn the feed on.
- **Analysis is directional.** Compare dones credited to push vs. feed touchpoints (attribution rule above) and the week 1-2 vs. 3-4 done-rate, knowing novelty decay and a growing backlog confound it. With ~35 users, splitting into randomized groups would be too small to mean much, so no causal claim is made. **Decision it drives:** if feed-credited dones are at least comparable to push-credited dones, the feed gets the next build cycle. Otherwise the next cycle goes into push and groups.

## Open Questions

- Which city? (Pick the one where the densest friend group lives and where the founder can recruit 20+ people by name.)
- Does the Instagram data export reliably include saved-post URLs, and is bulk import worth adding as an onboarding "whoa" moment?
- Which places provider? Criteria: storage and caching terms (hard requirement), cost per lookup, and coverage. Options: Google Places, Foursquare, Apple MapKit search.
- How should notifications be capped? Start at ≤1/day and ≤3/week, then tune.
- What's the confidence threshold for asking vs. auto-pinning? Set it from the Phase 0 matrix.
- Final product name ("Experience" is hard to trademark and search for).
- Android: deferred. That limits the stranger cohort to iPhone users.

## Success Criteria

- **Active user:** in a given period, saved at least 1 item or opened the app. Results count only if there are at least 20 active users in week 4.
- **North star:** experiences marked done per active user per month. Target to beat during validation: at least 1 done per active user per month in the friend cohort, and at least half that rate in the stranger cohort. (If strangers come in far below friends, the friends' numbers are being inflated.)
- **Habit:** saves per active user per week stay above zero through week 4.
- **Identification:** for TikTok/YouTube, at least 70% of saves need no correction. For Instagram, at least 80% are resolved by the inline question in under 10 seconds.
- **Push vs. pull:** dones credited to reminders, the digest, and the feed (attribution rule in Phase 1), reported as directional.
- **Kill signal:** after 4 weeks, with at least 20 active users, fewer than 0.25 dones per active user per month across the cohort, while people are still saving. That means resurfacing isn't changing behavior. Stop and rethink before building groups or discovery.

## Distribution Plan

- iOS only. TestFlight for validation (external testers need TestFlight beta review; plan a few days for it).
- Backend (Eng review D3): **one Supabase project** containing Auth (Sign in with Apple), Postgres with row-level security, Edge Functions `identify` and `digest`, a `jobs` table for queueing, and `pg_cron` for scheduling. There is no separate API server. The iOS app and share extension share one local Swift package, `ExperienceCore` (models, API client, outbox, auth).
- CI: GitHub Actions for backend tests; Xcode Cloud or fastlane for TestFlight builds. App Store release only after the validation gate passes.

## Dependencies

- Apple Developer account ($99/yr) for TestFlight and the share extension.
- Places API key and billing.
- LLM API for extraction.
- Phase 0 results gate the identification design and the feed design.

## The Assignment

**Run a concierge test this week, while Phase 0 spikes run.** Write down 20 friends in your densest city by name, plus 5-10 strangers from a local subreddit or Discord. Ask each to forward you every reel they'd want to do, by DM or text, for two weeks. You identify each place by hand (that same effort fills your platform matrix). Every Thursday evening, text each person "3 of your saves near you this weekend," and ask them to reply "done" when they go. At the end of two weeks you'll have the first real done-rate this idea has ever had, before the real app build starts. (The Phase 0 spikes are throwaway.) This is Gate 1, applied exactly as defined under "Gates" in the Recommended Approach. If it's zero, you've saved yourself months.

## What I noticed about how you think

- When I asked what made you actually go to something you'd saved, you said "I rarely actually do them." Most founders won't admit that their own product's core behavior is something they don't do. That honesty is exactly what reshaped the product toward resurfacing.
- You pushed back on "the nudge matters more than the map" with a picture, not an argument: "Imagine being able to scroll through a map... These scrolls are your saved posts." That feed is the most original idea in this session and the clearest thing setting you apart from eight competitors.
- You said you're "curious to see how users use it since there are so many things it can encompass." That curiosity is the right instinct. Point it at the data: the category that keeps turning into visits is your wedge.
- You chose the native app over my recommendation. Own that choice by doing the risk spikes first, so the build is informed by evidence rather than a bet.

### R3-1 — consistency (blocking)

**Problem**

> The new persisted-vs-fetched rule caches lookup fields including place_name, hours and price for a default of 30 days.
